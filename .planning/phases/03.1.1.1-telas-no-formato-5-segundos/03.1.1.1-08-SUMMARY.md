# Phase 03.1.1.1 Plan 08: Fechamento do mês Summary

**"Fechamento do mês" opens from the Resultados verdict as a full-screen story of up to 9 one-number cards (abertura, alcance, seguidores, post campeão, custo por conversa, WhatsApp, onde mais perdemos, 3 apostas, resumo), each compared only with the clinic's own previous period with the dates written on the card, driven by tap, swipe, keys and buttons, honouring reduced motion, with nothing to share, export or send.**

## What was built

- **Entry:** a "Fechamento do mês" button in the `acao` slot of the Resultados verdict (doctor and team alike). Period of 28 to 31 days opens the story directly; any other period navigates to the shell's own `30d` preset and opens the story once the data has loaded (button `title`: "O fechamento usa os últimos 30 dias").
- **Player:** native `<dialog>` with `showModal`, body scroll locked and restored, focus returns to the entry button (also after the reload to `30d`). Progress bars, "Cartão N de M" for screen readers, `aria-live` card, buttons Anterior / Próximo / Pausar / Sair at 44 px. Tap on the left 30% goes back, elsewhere forward; drag over 40 px; hold over 450 ms only pauses; ArrowLeft/ArrowRight, Space (pause), Esc. Auto-advance every 7 s with the bar filling and numbers counting up only when `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; with `reduce` there is no timer, no count-up, no transition and no Pausar button. Last card: "Ver de novo" and "Sair" only.
- **Cards (each only with measured data; never a 0 for missing data):** abertura (Dra./Dr. name when known), alcance (sum of closed days, dots on one scale for before and now, "1 ponto = N pessoas"), seguidores novos (per-day comparison, followers line), post campeão (most saves; caption as text with 2-line clamp and the full text in `title`; reach, format, theme, weekday and hour in São Paulo), custo por conversa (only with at least `LIMITES.funil.minLeads` conversations in both periods, "N% mais barato|caro", own days on a ruler), WhatsApp (daily bars), onde mais perdemos (maior perda of the drawn funnel with mini bars and "fecharam N, antes M"), 3 apostas, resumo final.
- **Shared rule with Hoje:** `montarEntradaDaHoje`, `descobertaDaAcao` and `fontesDoCliente` now live in `src/lib/leitura/entrada-da-hoje.ts`; the Hoje route calls them (Hoje output unchanged: `hoje.spec`, `cinco-segundos-hoje.spec`, `test-leitura-hoje` green).
- **Data read by Resultados:** `["client", clientId]` and `["client-sync-status", clientId]` (the same keys Hoje and the shell use, one small read each when cold) and nothing else new. The cards are built only while the dialog is open.

- **Commit:** `4980e5e`

### Deviations that need João or the orchestrator

**2. `entrada-da-hoje.ts` exports more than the plan listed.** Besides `DadosDaEntradaDaHoje` and `montarEntradaDaHoje` it exports `descobertaDaAcao` (moved from the Hoje route) and `fontesDoCliente` (moved with its helper). Without `descobertaDaAcao` the apostas could not be provably the same sentence as Hoje's findings. Hoje imports all three; `fatosDePublicacoes` is no longer referenced in the Hoje route (acceptance: 0).

**3. The entry button only exists when the Resultados verdict is in "veredito" mode.** With the verdict loading, in error or "Falta dado" there is no button (the `acao` slot is only drawn in the verdict mode). The story needs data to be worth opening, but a clinic with no verdict has no way into the closing.

**4. "Alcance" is the sum of the daily reaches** (the same number Resultados already labels "soma dos dias"), so the same person counts again on each day. The card says "de alcance no Instagram, somando os dias" and the dot scale says "1 ponto = N pessoas" as the plan asks. For periods up to 30 days the Meta unique reach exists (`periodoIg`); I did not use it because it has no previous-period twin to compare with.

**5. Pilot cards not built as in the pilot:** the "~N conversas a menos" estimate (D-05), Compartilhar and Baixar PDF (D-04), and the pilot's fixed 9-card list (cards without data are simply absent). The apostas title is the action phrase of the Hoje rule ("Revisar Post de foto na grade das próximas semanas"), not the pilot's hand-written titles.

**6. Focus ring on the frame.** After opening the dialog the focus goes to the frame (so Space and the arrows work without pressing a button); at 1440 a blue focus ring is visible around it once the keyboard is used.

**7. hoje.spec (c) and (f)** were red in plan 03's run; in this base they are green (76 of 76), so no edit to `hoje.spec.ts` was needed.
