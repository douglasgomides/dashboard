## Deviations from Plan

### Plan interpretation choices

**1. The fila warnings (CmAviso) stay visible, in `corpo`, not inside Ver tudo.** The plan says everything moves into Ver tudo unchanged. A "Fila esquecida" alert is a warning, so it is not hidden; it appears only when the queue read says so.

**2. CmTopo is gone.** Its question became the page header (`O atendimento deu conta das conversas?`) and its sync button moved to the header (desktop) and to the top of Ver tudo (phone), as one more block, unchanged.

**3. Closed days only.** The band, the totals, the Instagram reach and the ad conversations all stop at the last closed day (the same rule Pacientes uses). The old "Volume por dia" chart inside Ver tudo still shows every day.

**4. One extra read the plan's table does not list:** `["wts-resumo", clientId, start, fimFechado]` (one row). "Pessoas diferentes" must be counted over the same closed-day window as the previous period; when the period ends before today the key equals the existing `wts-resumo` read, so nothing extra is fetched. The previous-window reads use `periodosDeComparacao(...).anterior`, the same window the shared `resultados-diarios` keys use.

**5. Pessoas diferentes has no 30-day line** (distinct people have no per-day series). "Viram lead de cada 100" has a per-day rate line; both lines are hidden on the phone for the height ruler.

**6. NxSelo "Em alta/Em queda" is not repeated on the number cards** when the pill already carries sign and word (same call as plans 03 and 05). "Fora do normal" is not used on a card.

**7. Weekday finding uses the mean per weekday** (as the plan says). A very high day (the Instagram peak) lifts its weekday's mean; the finding's "Por quê?" says it is an average. A median would be more robust; question for João.

**8. Initial crossed day only on wide screens** (phone opens with the hint). The plan allowed this when the height requires it.

**9. Findings 'impacto' chip hidden in narrow findings** (3 side by side on desktop, and on the phone). The same number is in the "Por quê?" bars.

**10. Specs beyond the plan's list:** `comercial-painel.spec.ts` (in the plan) and the new spec only. `telas-juntadas.spec.ts` and `crm-kanban.spec.ts` ran unchanged and passed.
