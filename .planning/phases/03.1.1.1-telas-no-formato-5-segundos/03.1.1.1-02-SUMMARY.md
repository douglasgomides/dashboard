# Phase 03.1.1.1 Plan 02: Nota do dia and the stage-vs-before proof on Hoje Summary

**Hoje's verdict is now the nota do dia (a 0-100 ring plus the word of the band and one sentence, by the D-03 rule, explained on tap and always labelled "Regra do Nexo, não garante resultado"), the proof is each funnel stage against the clinic's own previous period with "aqui virou" at the turning point, and "Ver tudo" gained a numbers-against-before table.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 150 min
- **Tasks:** 3 (1 tracer, 1 tdd auto, 1 auto)
- **Files:** 9 touched (7 created, 2 modified), all inside the plan's `files_modified` (`hoje.spec.ts` needed no edit, see below)

## Accomplishments

- `nota-do-dia.ts`: `pontosDoFator(d) = clamp(round(50 + d/2), 0, 100)`, `notaDoDia` averages the factors with reliable data, a factor with no previous period, a zeroed previous or a base below its minimum goes to `faltaDado` with the reason (`base pequena: 4 de 5`), and with no factor the nota is null.
- `cinco-hoje.ts`: the 5 D-03 factors in order (conversas, custo por conversa, seguidores novos, fecharam, leads atendidos) with the pinned bases (`LIMITES.funil.minLeads` for conversas and the previous ad conversations, `LIMITES.leitura.grupoMinimo` for fecharam, `MIN_DIAS_SALDO` measured days for followers); leads atendidos is always "O período anterior de pessoas não é medido". `vereditoDaHoje` writes one sentence of at most 90 characters that passes `checarFraseCinco` and the Hoje text guard.
- `NxNotaDoDia`: ring button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, title "Toque para ver o que puxou a nota"), arc and count-up only without reduced motion, panel with ▲/▼ factors, "Igual a antes", the striped "Falta dado" list, the band chips (current one marked) and the rule.
- `NxEtapasContraAntes`: row buttons with diverging bars, ▲/▼ plus the percent as text, a dashed "aqui virou" line, axis labels, a fixed line on tap and an accessible summary.
- Hoje route: two `ads_resumo` reads (one aggregate row each, keys `["ads-resumo", clientId, start, comparacao.fimFechado]` and `["ads-resumo", clientId, comparacao.anterior.start, comparacao.anterior.end]`, `staleTime` 5 min, `retry` false) only for the cost factor; failure turns just that factor into "Falta dado". The wait for the stable list uses `isLoading` for them so a failed read never blocks the page.

1. Ring: 168 to 144 px on desktop (clamp 120-144), 96 px beside the sentence at 560 px or less (ladder a).
2. Proof rows: 40 to 32 px on desktop, 22 px on phone, track 28 to 22 px (14 px on phone), title 14 px on one line on phone, the "Cada etapa contra o período anterior" line only for screen readers on phone, panel padding 16 and gap 8 (ladder c).
3. Verdict on phone: padding 8 px, sentence 18 px / 22 px, label gap 4 px (it must fit three lines of up to 90 characters).
4. Findings on cards 641 px or wider (Hoje only, scoped to `.nx-hoje`): the action and "Por quê?" sit on the same row as the text instead of a stacked column, 504 to 348 px for the three findings. This was not on the plan's ladder; it was cheaper than touching the other rungs and it hides nothing.
5. Band chips live only inside the ring panel from the start (ladder b); three findings stay visible (ladder d floor, no change).

The phone case with a turning point has only 5 px of margin (1213 of 1218). Real findings with longer sentences than the mocks will add height; the margin is thin on purpose, flagged for João.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Seguidores novos compared totals of periods with different numbers of closed days**
- **Found during:** Task 1, designing `fatoresDaHoje`
- **Issue:** the plan says "seguidores-novos = crescimento.novos"; but `analyzeGrowth` only counts closed days, and the current period usually has fewer than the previous one, so the total would read as a drop that did not happen.
- **Fix:** the factor compares the average per measured day (both periods need `MIN_DIAS_SALDO` measured days) and the line reads "11 por dia contra 11 por dia".
- **Files modified:** `src/lib/leitura/cinco-hoje.ts` (also the "Ver tudo" table row "Seguidores novos por dia")
- **Commit:** `0d6038f`, `5f39642`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] A 1% change showed "▲ 1%" and counted as growth in the proof**
- **Found during:** Task 3, first screenshot
- **Issue:** the key numbers on top call anything under 3% "= igual"; the proof contradicted them and a 1% wiggle could create or break the turning point.
- **Fix:** `sentidoDaEtapa` (3% band) drives the text, the bar and `tituloDaVirada`.
- **Files modified:** `src/lib/leitura/cinco-hoje.ts`, `src/components/nx/etapas-contra-antes.tsx`, `scripts/test-cinco-hoje.ts`
- **Commit:** `5f39642`

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Heights over the limit after the new pieces (1317/1464 px)**
- See "Measured heights" for the compression steps. Step 4 (findings inline on wide cards) is outside the plan's ladder.
- **Files modified:** `src/styles/nexo-hoje.css`
- **Commit:** `1f65d14`

### Plan interpretation choices (not bugs)

- **Task 2 CSS shipped in Task 1's commit:** the whole `.nx-nota*` and `.nx-etapas*` block was written once and committed with the tracer (`0d6038f`); the later height tuning is in `1f65d14`. At `5f39642` the heights were therefore not yet inside the limit (only the final commit is).
- **`hoje.spec.ts` not edited:** it stayed green (159 passed with `telas-juntadas.spec.ts`) with nothing to update; the plan allowed updating only what moved. The nota and the proof are covered by the new spec.
- **`NxEtapasContraAntes` gained an `indiceDaVirada` prop** (the plan's props were `etapas`, `titulo`, `rotuloDoAnterior`) so the dashed line knows where to go.
- **Proof title and "mês antes":** the neutral title is the fixed text "Cada etapa comparada com o período anterior", while the fixed line on tap and the sub line use `rotuloDoAnterior(dias)` ("mês antes" for 28-31 days), as plan 01 does for the funnel.
- **"Ver tudo" summary text** changed from "Leitura, outras ações e a equipe" to "Todos os números do período" (the plan names it that way); the closed block now always exists because the table is always there.
- **Commit trailer:** `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5` (the model that ran), not Opus 5.5 as the brief asked.
