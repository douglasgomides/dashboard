## Deviations from Plan

### Plan interpretation choices

**1. Verdict support line reads "é o que mais alcança", not "mais funciona".** `melhorFormato` ranks by reach median and the Formato tab ranks by saves per post; "funciona" would have contradicted the tab. Same min-base rule (8 posts).

**2. Heatmap tab at 375 is over the ruler.** Plan truth: Desempenho "with Ver tudo closed" within 1218/1350, which is the default (Formato) state and is asserted for both profiles and both windows. Horário at 375 measures 1319 px; 7 tappable rows plus legend and sentence do not compress below that without removing the sentence or the legend. The e2e logs it and asserts everything else (Horário at 1440: 1348, Tema, Formato). Question for João below.

**3. 30-day lines are hidden on the number cards at 560 px or less** (kept on desktop), the same call plan 05 made for "Pessoas na mesa"; without it admin at 375 measured 1255 px.

**4. Impact chip, page label and decision/period line hidden on mobile** for the height ruler (the number in jogo is still in "Por quê?" and in the chip's `title`).

**5. Findings use the finding's own rule for "melhor".** Format: needs a different most-published format, 8+ posts each, 1.25x saves. Theme: 2+ themes with 8+ posts and 1.25x the account's average saves. Hour: cell with 2+ posts. The default mocks of `telas-juntadas` (30 posts, no theme) produce fewer than 3 findings and the page shows "Poucas descobertas neste período: ...", it never invents one.

**6. Top 5 by saves includes every format** (like the pilot), unlike the Destaques tab that splits posts and Reels. The 2-line caption clamp has the whole caption in `title`; the reach goes in the small label under the saves number instead of a chip (a third chip overflowed the 210 px card at 375, caught by the no-cut check).

**7. Spec edits beyond the plan's words.** `instagram-painel` (the /conteudo loop, the "sem projeção" test), `telas-juntadas` (the D-07 pílula test and the "3 telas juntadas" loop at line ~827, both for `conteudo`) and `blocos-nx` (Seguidores por semana now opens "Ver tudo") follow the new order. No honesty check was weakened: each still asserts the same thing, now after opening "Ver tudo" or on the verdict.
